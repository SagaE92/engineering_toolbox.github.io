Spec 55 wire size

Hookup (55A011X): lightweight single-wall 0.006", 12–30 AWG, for equipment/internal wiring.
Airframe (55A081X): dual-wall 0.010", 26–00 AWG, more robust for harnesses and larger sizes.

Length is the total conductor length (supply + return); no automatic doubling.

R at temp = R20 × (1 + 0.00393 × (T − 20)). Drop = I × R × length. Spec 55 continuous rating –65 °C to 200 °C (silver/nickel) or 150 °C (tin). Voltage-drop targets: critical / electronics ≤ 3–5 %; general power or weight-sensitive high-load runs can accept 10–15 % (lost power is the trade-off). Ampacity is not calculated.

Wavelength and antenna

λ = c / f with c = 299 792 458 m/s. Velocity factor scales the antenna lengths only. Practical wire dipoles are cut about 0.95 × the free-space ½ λ (end effect); coax VF is typically 0.66–0.85.
